Community Outreach Ministries Department

As a church family, we are committed to being the hands and feet of Jesus in Harlow and beyond. The Community Outreach Ministries Department focuses on practical service, showing Christ’s love in action and meeting the needs of those around us. For 2025–2026, our two main outreach initiatives are:

Community Outreach

Our Community Outreach Ministry is at the heart of service in action—engaging with the needs of the local population through charitable events, practical assistance, and meaningful interactions. From distributing essential supplies and providing free health services to offering workshops that empower and uplift, the ministry exemplifies Christ’s love through genuine, hands-on compassion. It is a bridge between the church and the community, helping build stronger, more connected lives.

Outreach & Prison Ministries Incharge

Monthly Food Bank Collection

  • On the first Sabbath of every month, we will collect non-perishable food items for the local food bank.
  • Donations will support families in need across Harlow, providing hope and relief during challenging times.
  • Every member is encouraged to bring at least one item each month—together, small contributions will make a big difference.

Clothes Collection and Distribution

  • Throughout the year, we will collect gently used clothes for men, women, and children.
  • These items will be distributed through local charities working directly with individuals and families in need.
  • Clean, wearable clothing donations are especially appreciated—help us bring warmth, dignity, and care to our community.

Why We Do This

“Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.” (Matthew 25:40)

Our mission is simple: to share God’s love in practical ways, showing compassion and kindness to those who need it most.

How You Can Get Involved

Mark your calendar for the monthly food bank collection Sabbath.

Gather clothes at home and bring them to church for donation.

Pray for those who will receive these gifts, that they may also experience God’s love.

✨ Together, we can make a difference in Harlow—one bag of food, one item of clothing, one act of kindness at a time. ✨
